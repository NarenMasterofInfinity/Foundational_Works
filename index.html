THE APPROACH

Read an idea.
Change an input.

Understanding gets stronger when you can connect a story to the evidence—and then try the mechanism for yourself.

01

Start with the question.

Learn what a method was trying to solve, how it developed, and why the earlier ideas still matter.

02

Keep the sources close.

Original papers, clear explanations and useful perspectives sit beside the ideas they support, ready for a deeper read.

03

Make the mechanism visible.

Worked examples and interactive experiments turn a formula into something you can inspect. Teaching assumptions stay visible.
